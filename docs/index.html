Feature set
Music

Every player, same controls

System-wide Now Playing. Apple Music, Spotify, a browser tab, a podcast app: one transport with artwork, a scrubber, shuffle and repeat.

Drop

A temporary file shelf

Drag files at the notch and it opens to catch them. Drag them back out one at a time or all at once. Items expire on their own.

Notes

Plain quick notes

A plain scratchpad in the notch. Open it, type, close it. Notes keep themselves saved.

Focus

A pomodoro that stays put

A running timer keeps a progress ring and live countdown parked at the far left of the notch, closed or open.

Updates

Autoupdates via Sparkle

Signed updates, checked and installed in place. No account, no launcher, no nagging.
